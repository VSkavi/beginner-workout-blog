Do You Need Equipment at All? (Start with Bodyweight First)

No. A complete 20-minute routine — squats, push-ups, glute bridges, core work — needs nothing but floor space, and it's exactly where you should start before spending a single rupee. Equipment makes a workout more efficient once bodyweight alone stops being challenging; it doesn't make a beginner workout more effective on day one.

Tip

Resistance bands vs. dumbbells for beginners: start with the band. It's cheaper, safer to learn form with, and covers more of a beginner circuit than a single pair of dumbbells can. Add dumbbells once band-resisted squats and bridges start feeling too easy.

What to Skip as a Beginner

These get marketed hard at beginners specifically, but none of them earn their price or floor space until you've outgrown everything above.

  • ✕
    TreadmillsExpensive, heavy, and space-hungry — a walk outside does the same job for free.
  • ✕
    Smart mirrorsA premium product solving a problem — form-checking and motivation — a phone propped against a wall already solves for free.
  • ✕
    Multi-gymsBuilt for someone who's already outgrown dumbbells and bands. A beginner won't use its full resistance range for months, if ever.
