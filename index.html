Short answer

Stand on any clear patch of floor, follow the 5-move circuit below for 20 minutes, twice a week to start. That's the entire beginner program — everything past this point is just detail and support.

Why bodyweight, why beginners

Difficulty scales with you — not the other way around.

Squats, push-ups and bridges use your own weight as resistance, so there's no equipment to buy, calibrate, or put away. Most beginner guides skip straight to the workout and assume you already know what a "circuit" or a "set" is. This one doesn't.

  • Every move has a built-in easier option
  • Every term explained the first time it's used
  • Nothing to set up, nothing to put away
  • Works on carpet, a mat, or a folded towel

The 20-minute structure

Three phases. One square of floor.

3 min

Warm-up

Marching, arm circles, torso twists, ankle rolls — wakes the body up before load.

14 min

Main circuit

5 moves, 40 sec work / 20 sec rest, repeated for 2–3 rounds: squat, incline push-up, standing march, glute bridge, dead bug.

3 min

Cooldown

Quad and hamstring stretches, child's pose, slow breathing — brings your heart rate back down.

Before you start

Rushing through reps, holding your breath, and skipping the warm-up or cooldown. Slow down, breathe out on the effort, and don't skip the 3-minute warm-up.

Twice a week is a strong, sustainable starting point. Once that feels easy, add a third session.

It burns calories, and more importantly builds the habit of moving consistently — pair it with realistic eating and enough sleep for the best results.
